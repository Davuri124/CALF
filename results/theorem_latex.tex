
\begin{theorem}[CALF Identification]
\label{thm:identification}
Let $\mathcal{X} = (\mathcal{R}, \mathcal{S}, \mathcal{L}, \mathcal{C})$ denote the
tree-structured covariate space of Roots, Soil, Leaves, and Choice respectively.
Under the following assumptions:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{A\arabic*}]
    \item \textbf{Consistency (SUTVA):} $Y_i = Y_i(T_i)$ for all $i$
    \item \textbf{Strong Ignorability:}
          $\{Y(0), Y(1)\} \perp T \mid (\mathcal{R}, \mathcal{S}, \mathcal{L})$
    \item \textbf{Overlap:} $0 < \mathbb{P}(T=1 \mid \mathcal{X}) < 1$ for all $\mathcal{X}$
    \item \textbf{Model Consistency:} The CALF encoder $f_\theta$ is a
          universal approximator on compact subsets of $\mathcal{X}$
\end{enumerate}
the Individual Treatment Effect $\tau_i = Y_i(1) - Y_i(0)$ is nonparametrically
identified from the observational distribution $\mathbb{P}(Y, T, \mathcal{X})$ via
the backdoor adjustment formula, and the CALF estimator $\hat{\tau}_i$ is
consistent as $n \to \infty$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof Sketch]
By A2 (strong ignorability), the potential outcomes are independent of treatment
given the observed pre-treatment covariates $(\mathcal{R}, \mathcal{S}, \mathcal{L})$.
Combined with A3 (overlap), the ATE is identified by the backdoor formula
$\tau = \mathbb{E}[\mathbb{E}[Y \mid T=1, \mathcal{X}] - \mathbb{E}[Y \mid T=0, \mathcal{X}]]$.
By A4, CALF's tree-structured encoder with multi-head cross-layer attention
is a universal approximator, guaranteeing that the potential outcome functions
$\mu_t(\mathcal{X}) = \mathbb{E}[Y(t) \mid \mathcal{X}]$ can be approximated
arbitrarily well for $t \in \{0, 1\}$ as model capacity increases.
Consistency of $\hat{\tau}_i$ follows from consistency of the potential outcome
estimators $\hat{\mu}_0$ and $\hat{\mu}_1$ under A1--A4. $\square$
\end{proof}
